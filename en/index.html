MATCHDAY 01The match

Not a replay. The match.

Your team's matches always play out live, minute by minute. And when it matters most, the ball lands at your feet: three options, ten seconds on the clock, and if your nerve fails, the cautious call plays itself. Try it here — it's the exact dilemma you'll face on the pitch.

MATCHDAY 02The career

You don't play the match. You play the decade.

Seasons pass, youngsters bloom, thirty-somethings lose their stride. The market calls, your scouts lie to you less over time, and the trophy cabinet fills up — or it doesn't.

Market with counter-offers

Summer and winter windows, AI clubs trading among themselves, a director of football who haggles: you. Low-ball offer? Counter at full price.

Special abilities

Bomber d'area, Ghiaccio nelle vene, Fuoriclasse. Three rarities, real effects on the pitch — and at most three legends per league.

Scouts & Primavera

Hidden potentials, estimates that sharpen as you upgrade your scouts. Gems exist — finding them is a different sport.

Match length, your call

1-2 minutes compressed, 5-6 relaxed, 7-8 with extended commentary. Or "skip to the result": that's a style choice too.

Injuries & decline

Injuries happen, and sooner or later an over-30's pace vanishes. The Divisione d'Onore doesn't forget.

Shareable seeds

The same league, regenerated from the seed: challenge a friend on the exact same world. "Let me send you my league" becomes a thing you say.

MATCHDAY 04Abilities

Not all champions are created equal.

Every player can be born with an ability. Commons make the difference, rares win matches, the legendary one… that's the page you write.

Commons

Bomber d'area · Falcata · Muro di Gomma · Maratoneta. The bread of any squad: they show up more often — and they're never enough.

Rares

Ghiaccio nelle vene · Regista · Capitano · Specialista di punizioni · Falco aereo. They swing the big matches.

Legendary

Fuoriclasse. Double impact on goals and team strength. Max 3 per league: once you've seen one, you don't forget it.
