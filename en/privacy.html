Privacy, in short

Pitchtory is an entirely client-side football manager. In other words: privacy here isn't a promise, it's the architecture.

01 Everything stays on your device

Careers, squads, settings and preferences are saved in your browser (IndexedDB and localStorage). There are no accounts and no remote databases: your data never leaves your device.

02 No trackers

No profiling cookies, no behavioural analytics, no advertising, no social pixels. Neither the landing site nor the app send data to third parties.

03 What the hosting sees

The pages are static and served by Cloudflare Pages, which processes standard HTTP requests (IP address, requested URL, user agent) to make the site work and for security — like any web server. We don't use that data to profile you: we have no use for it.

04 Deleting everything

Clearing the site's data from your browser settings deletes everything, permanently. There is no copy elsewhere — we don't exist anywhere else.

05 Questions

For any question, open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
